# Komunity Marketing Strategy
> *Your community. Transparent. Safe. Together.*

---

## 🧭 Platform Summary

**Komunity** is a transparent group wallet & community finance platform built for African communities — specifically stokvels, burial societies, churches, sports clubs, and professional associations. It eliminates money drama by making every rand visible to every member.

**Key differentiators:**
- Full financial transparency (every member sees every transaction)
- No bank account needed (voucher top-ups from Shoprite, Pick n Pay; mobile money withdrawals)
- Biometric security
- Fundraising / emergency campaigns
- WhatsApp-native onboarding

**Target markets:** South Africa → Zimbabwe, Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana → African diaspora in UK & USA

---

## 🎯 Target Audiences (Personas)

| Persona | Profile | Core Pain | What Komunity Fixes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stokvel Admin** | 30–55 yo woman, runs 8–20 person savings group | Chasing contributions every month, trust disputes | Auto-reminders, transparent ledger |
| **Burial Society Chair** | 40–65 yo community elder | Treasurer fraud fears, manual record-keeping | Multi-sig wallet, full audit trail |
| **Church Treasurer** | 35–60 yo, faith leader | Congregation demanding financial accountability | Real-time reporting, campaign tracking |
| **Sports Club Manager** | 25–45 yo, amateur or semi-pro | Collecting membership fees from 30+ players | Mobile pay, instant dashboard |
| **Student Society Leader** | 18–25 yo, university | Stolen funds from dishonest treasurer | Member-visible ledger, no single-point control |
| **Diaspora Group Leader** | 28–50 yo, UK/USA-based | Managing money for community back home | Cross-border, multi-currency support |

## 📱 Channel Strategy

### 1. WhatsApp Marketing (🔥 Highest Priority)
WhatsApp is the operating system of African community life. This is your #1 channel.

**Tactics:**
- **WhatsApp Business API** — Send contribution reminders, campaign updates, and onboarding flows directly in WhatsApp
- **Viral invite links** — "Share via WhatsApp" is already on your waitlist page. Make the message more compelling:
  > *"I'm tired of the drama at stokvel. Found this → komunity.app"*
- **WhatsApp Communities** — Build an official Komunity Community for admins to share tips, success stories, and peer support
- **Status content** — Create shareable WhatsApp status cards (transparent, branded) showing group savings milestones
- **Referral chain** — "Share with 3 people from your stokvel, skip to the front of the waitlist"

**Tools:** WhatsApp Business API (Meta for Developers), WhatsApp Cloud API, Wati.io or Respond.io

---

### 2. Social Media

#### Facebook (Priority: High)
> Facebook groups ARE African community digital life — burial societies, stokvels, and churches all have active groups.

- **Targeted Facebook Ads** targeting:
  - Interests: Stokvel, burial society, savings group, church community
  - Behaviours: Mobile money users, Shoprite shoppers
  - Demographics: SA women 30–55, township & peri-urban areas
- **Facebook Group seeding** — Post value-first content in existing stokvel & burial society groups (not spam — genuine advice)
- **Facebook Page** with trust-building content: "5 signs your stokvel treasurer is hiding money" type educational posts
- **Retargeting** — Pixel on waitlist page, retarget everyone who visited but didn't sign up

#### TikTok (Priority: Medium-High)
> Viral potential is huge. "Stokvel drama" content gets millions of views.

- **Relatable drama videos** — Recreate common stokvel/burial society conflict scenarios, then show how Komunity solves it
- **Stitch / Duet** popular "money drama" content from creators
- **Creator partnerships** — SA personal finance creators: @moneywithmofe type accounts
- **"Day in the life of a stokvel admin"** — Before/After with Komunity

#### Instagram (Priority: Medium)
- **Reels** repurposed from TikTok
- **Aesthetic infographics** — "12 million South Africans are in stokvels. Zero have a transparent app. Until now."
- **Story highlights** for testimonials, features, and how-it-works

#### LinkedIn (Priority: Low-Medium)
- **Thought leadership** — "Why African community finance is an underserved $50B market"
- Target professional associations, HR professionals at corporates (employee stokvels)
- Reach investors and fintech ecosystem

**Campaign 3: Conversion (FOMO)**
- Headline: *"First 500 groups get 6 months FREE"*

- CTA: Claim Your Spot

**Targeting:**
- Primary: SA women 28–55, township & suburban
- Secondary: Men 25–50 running clubs/associations
- Tertiary: African diaspora in UK/USA (18–55)

**Budget recommendation:**
- Start small: R2,000–R5,000/month to test creatives
